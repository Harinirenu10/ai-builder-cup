    How to Measure

    • 1

      Chest

      Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape parallel to the floor. The tape should be snug, but not too tight.

    • 2

      Arm

      With your arm bent slightly, measure from the base of the center back of your neck, along your shoulder, then over your elbow to your wrist bone.
