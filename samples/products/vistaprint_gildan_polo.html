    Grab our custom Gildan® polo shirt for your business.

    • 177 gsm, 100% lightweight cotton
    • 90% Cotton, 10% Polyester (Sports Grey)
    • Contoured welt collar with 4 buttons
    • Slim fit polo shirt
    • Sizes S to 2XL
    • Also available in women’s style

    Decoration: Embroidery (Up to 12 thread colours. Textured, slightly raised feel. Durable, professional look)

    Care instructions: Turn your polo shirt inside out and machine wash on cold. Don't bleach, tumble dry or dry clean. Avoid ironing the embroidered area.

    Note: This polo shirt is slim fit. For a looser fit, consider to size up.

    A staple piece Made from comfortable cotton fabric, our Gildan® Men’s Polo Shirt is the go-to addition to your wardrobe during sunny, hotter days – or even as a layering piece all-year round. It offers a stylish yet casual look, great for any occasion.

    A wearable promo Whether you’re looking to offer your loyal customers a stylish gift or you want to create a unique look for your employees, this shirt has got you covered. Your logo will be on full display, wherever people go – from the office to the golf course.

    Intuitive design studio Ready to create? Once you’ve selected a colour and size for your shirt, start adding your custom touches – your logo and text – using our design studio. Then, we will professionally embroider the shirts and have them delivered to you, ready to be worn.
